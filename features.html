Frequently asked questions

Is Ravio Music free?

Yes. Ravio Music is a free Android music player with no ads.

Does Ravio Music work offline?

Yes. You can download songs for offline playback and play audio files stored on your device.

Does Ravio have an equalizer?

Yes. It includes a Dual 10-Band equalizer plus bass boost, a loudness enhancer, a 3D virtualizer, pitch and tempo shift, and silence skipping.

Does Ravio Music work with Android Auto?

Yes. You can control your queue from Android Auto.

How do I install the Ravio APK?

Download Ravio.apk with the button on this site, open the file on your Android phone, and allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks. Then tap Install. Only download Ravio from this official site to be safe.

Can Ravio Music replace Spotify?

It can for many Android users. Ravio gives you free, ad-free playback, a Dual 10-Band equalizer, synced lyrics, offline caching and Android Auto support. Spotify has things Ravio does not, such as apps for iPhone and desktop, a very large set of editorial playlists and podcasts. If you listen mainly to music on Android, Ravio can be your main player. If you rely on those extras, you may want to keep both.

Is Ravio Music a free version of Spotify Premium?

No. Ravio is a separate app and is not affiliated with Spotify. Some of what it offers overlaps with what people pay for in a premium plan, such as no ads, offline listening and background playback, but its library, features and audio quality are its own and can differ from Spotify’s.

Which is better, Ravio Music or YouTube Music?

It depends on what you value. Ravio costs nothing, has no ads, and includes a 10-band equalizer, synced lyrics and AI mixes. YouTube Music has an official catalog with music videos, apps on more platforms, and account syncing, and its free tier is ad-supported while ad-free background playback and downloads generally need a paid Premium plan. If you want a free, ad-free Android player with strong sound controls, try Ravio.
