Privacy Policy.

Ravio Music is engineered with a strict local-first, zero-telemetry architecture. We do not run advertising networks, we do not track your listening behavior, and we do not operate backend servers that harvest your personal data.

Identity Notice: Ravio Music is the official rebrand and successor of EchoMusic, developed and maintained by Raman Chapagain.

Zero Data Harvesting

No analytics SDKs, crash-tracking spyware, or behavioral profiling scripts are bundled inside the Ravio APK.

SECTION 01

Overview & Transition from EchoMusic

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Ravio Music Android application (formerly distributed under the name EchoMusic) and its official website, developed by Raman Chapagain.

Ravio Music is a standalone, non-commercial, ad-free music streaming and local audio player client. Because we do not maintain centralized user accounts or proprietary cloud databases, we cannot view, access, or monetize your personal information.

SECTION 02

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data generated while using Ravio Music is stored locally inside your Android device's sandboxed application directory. This includes:

  • Library & Playback History: Liked tracks, custom playlists, local queue states, and listening stats used to power your on-device recommendations.
  • Audio & DSP Configuration: Your Dual 10-Band Equalizer curves, bass boost levels, pitch/tempo settings, and Material You / AMOLED UI preferences.
  • Offline Cache & Downloads: Audio streams and album artwork cached for offline playback remain strictly on your device storage.
  • Optional Streaming Authentication: If you choose to sign in with your third-party streaming account to sync playlists, your session cookies/tokens are stored locally using Android's encrypted storage and are only transmitted directly to the streaming provider.
SECTION 03

Ravio Brain AI & Live Translations

Ravio includes Ravio Brain AI to generate custom taste mixes, Vibe Summaries, Song DNA backstories, and real-time lyrics translations.

  • Zero PII Transmission: When you trigger an AI feature, the app sends only the relevant song metadata (track title, artist name, or lyric lines) required to fulfill the prompt.
  • No Background Profiling: AI requests are executed strictly on-demand when you interact with Ravio Brain features and are never linked to your personal identity, device ID, or contacts.
SECTION 04

Ravio Find (Audio Recognition & Microphone)

Ravio Music features Ravio Find, allowing you to identify music playing around you.

  • Strictly User-Initiated: Ravio only accesses your device's microphone when you explicitly tap the Ravio Find recognition button. It never listens in the background.
  • Ephemeral Audio Fingerprinting: A short audio snippet (typically 3–5 seconds) is converted into an acoustic fingerprint to match against public music databases and is immediately discarded once a match is returned.
SECTION 05

Third-Party Streaming & Lyrics APIs

To stream audio, fetch album artwork, and display time-synced lyrics, your device makes direct HTTPS requests to third-party APIs:

  • Music Streaming Services: Public media streams, search queries, and artist metadata are fetched directly from upstream streaming servers (such as YouTube Music / InnerTube endpoints).
  • Time-Synced Lyrics Providers: When playing a track, Ravio queries public lyrics databases (such as LRCLIB or Musixmatch) using only the track's title, artist, and duration.

Because your device connects directly to these endpoints to retrieve media, those services may see your standard network IP address just like any web browser. Ravio does not proxy, log, or intercept these requests.

SECTION 06

Android Device Permissions

Ravio requests only the minimum Android system permissions necessary to deliver uninterrupted audio playback and hardware integration:

Permission Technical Purpose in Ravio
INTERNET Streams audio, downloads updates, fetches synced lyrics, and queries Ravio Brain AI.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Ensures uninterrupted background music playback when your screen is off or while using Android Auto.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO Allows playing local MP3/FLAC/WAV audio files stored on your device and managing offline song downloads.
RECORD_AUDIO Optional. Used exclusively when you manually activate Ravio Find to identify a song.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays the persistent media playback notification with play, pause, and skip controls.
SECTION 07

Data Retention, Backups & Complete Deletion

You retain 100% ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Local Backup & Restore: You can export your database, playlists, and settings into a local backup file to transfer between devices, including migrating from older EchoMusic builds.
  • Instant Deletion: Clearing the app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling Ravio Music permanently and irreversibly erases all history, cached songs, and tokens from your device. Since we hold no copies on any server, nothing remains after uninstallation.
